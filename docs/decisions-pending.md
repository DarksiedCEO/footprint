Places where the build had to interpret or deviate from the approved spec.
Each is a config value or a one-line change; nothing here is hidden in code.

## Go-live gates

Footprint stays in paper until ALL of these hold. (1) Six consecutive weeks of paper trading at or above $25,000 net profit per week, counted from real IBKR paper fills only; one week below $25,000 resets the count. (2) The existing gates over the paper run: 200+ trades with 60+ out of sample, win rate >= 45%, payoff >= 1.5, profit factor >= 1.3, max drawdown <= 15%. The real-data backtest reports the actual distribution of winners; if $25,000/week is out of reach at one contract, position size is revisited with Andre. Going live is Andre's call only.

## Draw and compounding policy (live trading)

Live-trading withdrawal policy, decided by Andre; documentation only, no code effect; companion to the go-live gates. (1) Draws come only from a positive week; a flat or down week draws nothing. (2) The bankroll floor is $25,000, the starting capital: no draw may take the bankroll below it, so draws only ever come out of profit. (3) The account's health picks the draw: about $1,200 baseline on a modest green week (for example, a +$5,000 week draws about $1,200 and about $3,800 compounds); about $6,000 in steady state once Andre's obligations are cleared; about $20,000 catch-up only when a big week allows it, in the early phase. (4) Whatever is left after the draw compounds. Position size steps up as the bankroll grows (for example $25,000 to $30,000, later toward $10,000 per position), only at the same percent-of-bankroll risk, and every step-up is Andre's decision. (5) End state: grow the bankroll until the weekly targets come comfortably, without forcing or stretching any trade, and ultimately until the account reliably makes about $50,000-$60,000 a week. There, draws are optional: Andre may take about $20,000 now and then because he wants it in the bank, not because he needs it. There is no timeline for this; it depends on the real-data backtest and on live results that hold up.

## Order-path limits that need Andre's written sign-off (PR #9)

The order path never sells twice, never sells short and never books a guessed price. Two situations are deliberately left to Andre, because any automatic sale in them could go short (a short option has unlimited risk):

| # | Situation | What the system does | Worst case |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | The broker's position report will not hold still (it flickers or keeps changing) for 10 s | Sells nothing on the untrusted report; halts; latches the kill switch; alarms "flatten by hand" once; keeps checking and acts as soon as it settles | The position is held, protected only by its resting stop, possibly past the close |
| L2 | An order (a stop or an exit) was sent, its answer was lost, and the resend under the same id also failed, so nobody can say whether it is working | Counts it as working; sends nothing beside it; reconcile cancels and re-prices it every round and escalates "CANCEL STUCK ... flatten by hand" after 10 s | A long option held, possibly without a stop: the loss is limited to the premium paid |

L2 goes away in the IBKR PR with OCA groups (the broker itself guarantees only one of a position's stop and exits can fill).
